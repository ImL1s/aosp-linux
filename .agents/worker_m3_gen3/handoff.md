## 2. Logic Chain

1. **Solving Issue 1 (TOUCHPAD_MODE relative motion & gesture SGR encoding)**:
   - `TOUCHPAD_MODE` requires relative motion tracking rather than absolute touch positioning.
   - `SgrMouseProtocolGenerator.processTouchpadEvent(...)` maintains relative position accumulators `(mTouchpadAccumX, mTouchpadAccumY)` and updates simulated cursor coordinates `(mTouchpadCol, mTouchpadRow)` clamped to `[1, safeCols]` and `[1, safeRows]`.
   - Motion with dragging generates SGR button 32 packets, motion without drag generates SGR button 35 hover packets, single tap (<250ms & <20px move) generates button 0 press + release, and two-finger scrolling generates wheel buttons 64/65.
   - Both `TerminalView.java` and `TerminalSurfaceView.java` wire `onTouchEvent()` `case TOUCHPAD_MODE` into `processTouchpadEvent(...)` and forward the generated bytes to the PTY stream.

2. **Solving Issue 2 (Unwired Vsock Terminal Client output)**:
   - `TerminalView`'s `sendBytes()`, `sendFrame()`, and `sendResize()` methods previously only serialized frames and logged `Log.d` without sending bytes over vsock.
   - By calling `mVsockClient.sendFrame(frame)` inside a `try-catch (IOException)`, PTY input bytes, resize events, and touch escape sequences are transmitted over AF_VSOCK Port 5001.
   - `connectVsock()` enables bidirectional traffic by routing received vsock frames to `mVTermParser.processOutput(data)` and invoking `postInvalidate()`.
